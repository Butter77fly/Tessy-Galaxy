About Us

Where great food, vibrant culture, and unforgettable entertainment come together. At Tessy Galaxy, every visit is a celebration of Africa’s rich flavors, sounds, and spirit. Whether you're here for a delicious meal, to catch a live football match, enjoy a night of comedy and music, or simply relax with friends, you’ve come to the right place. Step inside and let the aromas, rhythms, and energy of Africa surround you. Welcome home. Tessy Galaxy is more than just a restaurant—it’s an experience. Founded with a passion for African culture and hospitality, we are committed to offering the very best in food, entertainment, and atmosphere. Our menu features a wide variety of authentic African delicacies, carefully prepared using traditional recipes and fresh, locally sourced ingredients. From jollof rice and suya to egusi soup, grilled fish, and more, every dish is made to satisfy both heart and soul. But Tessy Galaxy is also your go-to spot for fun and connection. We host regular live music, dance performances, stand-up comedy, talk shows, and themed nights that bring people together. And for sports lovers, our giant 8-by-5-foot HD screen makes every live match an exciting community event. Whether you’re dining, dancing, laughing, or watching the big game, Tessy Galaxy is the place to be. Come hungry. Leave happy.
